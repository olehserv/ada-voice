WPF fidelity notes

Mostly 1:1, two flags. ① The title-bar brand hairline uses CSS border-image; in WPF it is simpler — a 2 px Border with a LinearGradientBrush under the ui:TitleBar. ② The ribbon-widen hover animates scaleX, which in WPF must be a ScaleTransform on the marker Border (never animate Width). Glows are DropShadowEffect ShadowDepth=0, used only on the lamp dot and recording dot — cheap. The chunkier radii (10/14 px) and the always-red STOP need a WPF-UI button restyle + radius token change; the duration chip and numbered conversation rows are plain template edits. The red LIVE lamp reverses today's green-means-live convention — an owner decision to make consciously: broadcast-true and unmistakable, but it must be applied everywhere at once (pill, dot, docs) to avoid mixed signals.
